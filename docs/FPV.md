# FPV drone mode

Press **K** (rebindable, "FPV drone mode on / off") to turn the car into a first-person-view quadcopter with the flight model, controls and camera
of [ShramkoGSFPV](https://github.com/AndriiShramko/ShramkoGSFPV) (MIT).

## What is ported

| ShramkoGSFPV | RocketWorld |
|---|---|
| `packages/sim-core` `sim.ts`, `rates.ts`, `params.ts`, `wake.ts` | `Assets/Scripts/Sim/Fpv/*` (engine-free C#, `double` precision) |
| drone presets (`presets/*.json`) | `FpvPresets.cs`, generated by `tools/fpv/gen_presets.py`; **G** cycles them (9 drones, from a 1S whoop to a 4.8 kg cinelifter) |
| FPV camera: fixed to the frame, uptilt, 115 degrees **horizontal** FOV | `FpvCamera` (uptilt per preset, **-** / **=** change it by 5 degrees; near plane 0.1 m) |
| Mode 2 sticks, keyboard "pilot" layout, arm rule | `FpvInput` |
| crash / bounce / slide contact rules | same code, on PhysX queries through `FpvWorld` |

The model runs at its native **1 kHz** (8 or 9 steps per 120 Hz game tick): motor lag and battery sag, X mixer with airmode, Betaflight-style rate PID
(gyro and D-term filters, feed-forward, I-term relax, anti-windup), acro / horizon / angle, rotor-momentum drag, prop wash, crash detection.

Left out: wind (RocketWorld has none: still air), the invisible spawn platform, the lesson "slide" bump, record/replay, the DJI-style assists
(velocity / altitude hold) and radio calibration. Touch sticks are not wired for the drone.

## Controls

| | Keyboard (ShramkoGSFPV "pilot", Mode 2) | Gamepad (Mode 2) |
|---|---|---|
| throttle | **W / S** (stays where you leave it: 0 to 100 % in 0.5 s) | left stick up/down (springs to the centre = half power) |
| yaw | **A / D** (50 % stick) | left stick left/right |
| pitch / roll | **arrows** (60 % stick) | right stick |
| arm / disarm | **Space** | **A**, or push the throttle stick to the bottom |
| flight mode | **M**: angle, acro, horizon (starts in **angle**) | D-pad down |
| next drone | **G** | D-pad left |
| camera | **C**: FPV, chase, helicopter, orbit | Y |
| respawn | **R**: after a crash on the spot, else back to the launch point | Back |
| leave | **K** | (rebind in the Controls screen) |

Arming follows the simulator: the switch must go off to on **with the throttle at the bottom**; otherwise it refuses ("lower the throttle to arm").

## How it fits in the game

`FpvDriver` is a sibling of `PlaneDriver` / `VehicleDriver`: the car's Rigidbody becomes a pose carrier (no gravity, hit collider off) and the model writes its
pose into it after each tick, so interpolation, minimap, tile streaming and the chase camera follow the drone. After a Cesium origin shift the model
re-reads the pose from the body (`Resync`). Collision is the model's own, not PhysX's solver: `FpvWorld` sweeps the five body spheres with `Physics.SphereCast`
and resolves overlaps with a ray fan (the tile colliders are non-convex meshes, so `ComputePenetration` cannot be used). A broad `CheckSphere` per tick makes open sky free.

Frames: the model is right-handed with the nose along -z, Unity is left-handed with the nose along +z. `FpvFrame` is the only place that converts (z mirrored;
quaternion `(w, x, y, z)` to Unity `(-x, -y, z, w)`), so a level drone with identity attitude faces +z.

Multiplayer: like the Cessna, this mode is local; other players see the car model at the drone's pose.

## Verifying

```
SHRAMKO_DIR=/path/to/ShramkoGSFPV-main tools/fpv/run.sh
```
`gen_golden.mts` runs the original TypeScript model over 8 scripted flights (acro, angle and horizon, prop wash, free-fall crash, soft landing, a wall + floor corner that exercises the pinned-contact pass, a heavy preset, low gravity)
and `RwFpv` replays them on the C# port: every state slot, every 10-25 ticks, and the event sequence. Last result: **8/8, worst relative error 1e-10**.
`FpvModelTests` (EditMode) holds the behaviours that must never break (arming rule, parked until armed, hover stick on every preset, roll rate, crash on the floor).

**Not verified (no Unity in the sandbox where this was written):** the Unity compile of `Assets/Scripts/Runtime/Fpv/*` and of the edits to `RocketCar`, `GameRoot`, `CarInput`, `InputBindings`,
PhysX behaviour of `FpvWorld` against real tile colliders, and the feel at WebGL frame rates. `FpvWorld`'s logic was exercised against a stubbed floor.
